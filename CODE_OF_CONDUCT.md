# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

POLYON is a technical project intended for constructive collaboration. We are
committed to providing a welcoming environment where people can participate
regardless of background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and considerate;
- giving and receiving technical feedback professionally;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different technical perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation,
and other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates this code.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact
channel rather than creating a public issue.
